Project · since 1998–99

Swaasthya Kendra

Health care for people living in slums who lack easy access to government-run primary health centres, dispensaries and hospitals.

Our first community-based health centre, Swaasthya Kendra, was started in 1998–99. Its focus is primary medical care, basic maternal and child health, family planning, immunisation and referral services.

Swaasthya Kendra · Madanpur Khadar

Twice a week, doors open

The Swaasthya Kendra at Madanpur Khadar has run since 2007 and is our flagship health programme.

Its general OPD runs twice a week and treats common ailments: diarrhoea, fever, injuries, skin diseases, joint pains, asthma and upper respiratory tract infections.

Swaasthya Kendra · health education

Health education, every visit

Health education happens during the OPD, medical examinations and immunisation, and in prayer meetings, classes and the assembly hall.

Topics are chosen by what the community needs at the time: hygiene, environmental sanitation, food hygiene, hand washing, a balanced diet, and iodine deficiency disorders. It reaches people one by one, through parents’ groups, and across the community.

Project

School Health Program

A medical team of a Medical Officer and an ANM examines and checks the health of the students of Seth Vidyalaya and Project Savera.

The team also gives first aid and emergency care to children and staff.

School Health Program

A record for every child

Every child has a cumulative health record: health issues, treatment advice and follow-up.

Children who need a specialist are referred, and the team follows up referred cases on its monthly visits. When a child needs it, class teachers and parents are told about the child’s condition and advised on regular treatment and follow-up.
