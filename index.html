01 · Healthcare

Health care, close to home

Our first community health centre, Swaasthya Kendra, opened in 1998–99. The Swaasthya Kendra at Madanpur Khadar has run since 2007 and is our flagship health programme. Twice a week a medical officer treats common ailments there. Our School Health Program gave 1,192 schoolchildren check-ups and OPD care in 2025–26, and 5,736 people used the Swaasthya Kendra.

02 · Education

Two schools of our own

Seth Vidyalaya, a recognised primary school at Madanpur Khadar, teaches 655 students. Project Savera, in the same building in the afternoons, teaches 300 children from rag-picker and other poor families.

Our Scholarship Scheme for Meritorious Students helps 22 students carry on studying.

Your support for our mission will ensure that children from the underprivileged sections of society continue their Education.

More on education →

03 · Women development

Women development

Women development is one of our three areas of direct intervention, alongside Education and Healthcare Services.

With the Azim Premji Foundation we work against gender-based violence in seven communities of Delhi, reaching 14,958 people this year, with ten Mahila Panchayats, two Crisis Management Centres, Community Resource Centres and a weekly Legal Help Desk. And through Empowering Youth as Agents of Change, 153 young people learned a trade or English.

Since {{founded}}: {{years}} years of work

  1. 1992 Prayatn is founded by a group of people, to work with the disadvantaged sections of society.
  2. 1998–99 The first community-based health centre, Swaasthya Kendra, opens.
  3. 2007 Swaasthya Kendra at Madanpur Khadar begins. It is our flagship health programme today.
  4. 2017–18 The Scholarship Scheme begins, after two girls top class V but cannot afford the journey to their new school.
  5. 2023 The School Health Clinic opens for the students of Seth Vidyalaya and Project Savera, with Samrat Offset Pvt Ltd.
  6. 2025 Empowering Youth as Agents of Change begins: skills training and English for young people.
  7. Today Seven key projects, two schools with about 900 children, 22 scholars, ten Mahila Panchayats, and 14,958 people reached by our work with women.
