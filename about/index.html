Our mission

To empower the marginalized by building their capabilities through education and skill development for a decent, dignified and independent life.

Our vision

A society without economically weaker sections and disadvantaged, and where all are equal and active partners in the process of development.

Strategy

  • Capacity building
  • Community participation
  • Resource mobilisation
  • Networking

Approach

Holistic and participatory, involving all stakeholders.

Where we work

South and South-East Delhi

Our office is in Kalkaji. Our work reaches communities across the south and south-east of the city, and our project against gender-based violence runs in seven communities of South, South East and Central Delhi.

Milestones

  1. 1992 Prayatn is founded.
  2. 1998–99 The first community-based health centre, Swaasthya Kendra, opens.
  3. 2007 Swaasthya Kendra at Madanpur Khadar begins, today our flagship health programme.
  4. 2017–18 The Scholarship Scheme for Meritorious Students begins.
  5. 2023 The School Health Clinic opens, with Samrat Offset Pvt Ltd.
  6. 2025 Empowering Youth as Agents of Change begins.
  7. Today Seven key projects: Swaasthya Kendra, the School Health Program, Seth Vidyalaya, Project Savera, the Scholarship Scheme, Addressing Gender-Based Violence in Communities, and Empowering Youth as Agents of Change.

Board of Trustees

A Board of Trustees of eight members manages the affairs of Prayatn. It meets four times a year to review the organisation’s performance, including the role and work of the faculty and staff, to give suggestions, and to monitor each project’s progress against its plan of action.

At our schools

Parents at the table

School Management Committee

Eighteen members: parents’ representatives, teachers, trustees and members from educational institutions. It met three times in the year to review the school’s progress and plan the work ahead.

Parent Teacher Association

Parents, teachers and management together, to encourage parents to take part in school life, promote the all-round education of the children, and keep parents and teachers up to date on the school and its progress. It met on 11 November 2025 and chose a new parent representative for the committee, and each school held 12 parent-teacher meetings in the year.
