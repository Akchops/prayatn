Seth Vidyalaya

884 underprivileged children of Madanpur Khadar, aged 4 to 12, are getting quality primary education.

Since 2009 Prayatn has run Seth Vidyalaya, a co-educational primary school in Madanpur Khadar. It gives low-cost, quality education to children from poor and economically weaker sections of society, and helps them carry on with their education by mainstreaming them into government schools after Class V.

In October 2025 the Department of Education, MCD, granted Seth Vidyalaya recognition. As a result, 250 children of Project Savera, who had been studying in the afternoon shift in the same building, have joined Seth Vidyalaya.

The school ensures age-appropriate levels of learning leading to completion of the primary school cycle. It follows the MCD curriculum for the primary classes, with co-scholastic subjects (GK, art and craft, moral education and yoga) and co-curricular activities (music, dance, drama, declamation contests, quizzes, and sports and games) to bring out each child’s potential and build leadership and social skills.

Picnics and excursions are organised once a year for all students, and sports, games and health check-ups are an integral part of school life. Parent-teacher meetings are held every month. Students get extra help to improve their schoolwork through an after-school support programme, and are evaluated regularly through periodic class tests, subject enrichment activities, notebook marks, a half-yearly assessment and the annual examination.

Project · for meritorious students

Scholarship Scheme for Meritorious Students

26 students now receive Rs 12,000 a year to carry on studying.

It began in April 2017, when two girls topped their class V. They won places at Lady Irwin School for class VI, but their parents could not pay the transport to such a distant school. So the School Management Committee decided to give the toppers of class V Rs 1,000 a month for a year, towards fees, transport, uniform and books.

Every year since, four new students who come first or second are awarded the scholarship, and it is renewed each year for students who score more than 60% in their final results. Ten individual donors contribute to it every year.

Seth Vidyalaya · parents

Parents and the school

Parent-teacher meetings every month: 10 in the year, with 70% of parents taking part.

An eighteen-member School Management Committee of parents’ representatives, teachers, trustees and members from educational institutions met three times in the year to review the school’s progress and plan ahead. The Parent Teacher Association met on 11 November 2025 and chose a new parent representative for the committee.
