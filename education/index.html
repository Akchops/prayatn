Project

Seth Vidyalaya

Seth Vidyalaya is Prayatn’s school.

Your support for our mission will ensure that children from the underprivileged sections of society continue their Education.

Seth Vidyalaya · in class

In the classroom

Lessons at the blackboard, a computer lab, and reading in the library.

And craft tables covered in paper, clay and colour: paper wheels, paper houses, paper bicycles, cards for mothers.

Seth Vidyalaya · out of class

Out of the classroom

Morning exercise in the yard, sports day, and Republic Day in costume.

And trips out of school, like a visit to a science museum.

Seth Vidyalaya · health

Looked after

Seth Vidyalaya’s students are part of our School Health Program.

A Medical Officer and an ANM check their health, keep a record for every child, refer and follow up children who need a specialist, and give first aid and emergency care. More on the School Health Program →
