01 · Health

Health care, close to home

Our first community health centre, Swaasthya Kendra, opened in 1998–99. The Swaasthya Kendra at Madanpur Khadar has run since 2007 and is our flagship health programme.

02 · Education

A school of our own

Prayatn runs a school: reading, lessons at the blackboard, computers, craft and exercise in the yard.

Your support for our mission will ensure that children from the underprivileged sections of society continue their Education.

03 · Youth & women

Youth & women development

We work so that people can respond to and change their situation of inequality and injustice, with education and awareness, community involvement and collective action as the driving force of social and economic change.
