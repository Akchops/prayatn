About Prayatn

Working with the disadvantaged sections of society since {{founded}}

Prayatn is a registered Public Charitable Trust, founded in {{founded}} by a group of people and run from an office in Kalkaji, South Delhi. Our direct interventions are in the field of Education, Healthcare Services, Youth & Women Development.

Mission

To empower the marginalized by building their capabilities through education and skill development for a decent, dignified and independent life.

Prayatn exists to enable people to respond to and change their situation of inequality and injustice, with education and awareness, community involvement and collective action as the driving force of social and economic change.

How we work

We follow a Model Development Approach. It uses a mix of strategies to analyse a problem, understand its root causes, and develop and demonstrate solutions, addressing all dimensions of the problem at the same time.

Over {{years}} years we have planned, designed, implemented and managed development projects through a participatory approach.

Milestones

  1. 1992 Prayatn is founded.
  2. 1998–99 The first community-based health centre, Swaasthya Kendra, opens.
  3. 2007 Swaasthya Kendra at Madanpur Khadar begins, today our flagship health programme.
