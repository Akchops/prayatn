Education · our first priority

Every child in school

We try to meet the educational needs of all age groups, through formal education, non-formal education and our School Support Program.

  • Ensuring access to education.
  • Age-appropriate levels of learning.
  • Keeping children in school and preventing dropouts.

Our two primary schools, Seth Vidyalaya and Project Savera, gave quality primary education to about 900 children from economically disadvantaged families this year, and scholarships help the best of them carry on beyond primary school.

Project · supported by Deloitte through United Way of Delhi since April 2023

Seth Vidyalaya

655 students of the JJ cluster at Madanpur Khadar: 364 boys and 291 girls, from Nursery to Class V.

A recognised primary school giving low-cost, quality education, with well-trained teachers and the curriculum of MCD schools. Teachers use teaching and learning materials, science equipment and tablets; the youngest make their own flash cards, shapes and blocks. Library, games, IT, music and yoga are part of every week, and every student is assessed twice a year.

Seth Vidyalaya · in class

Learning by doing

27 Bal Sabhas this year: writing, drawing, poems, craft, paper-bag making, Show and Tell and GK quizzes.

Every day starts with an assembly where the older children read the news headlines, an amazing fact and the thought of the day. And every month, three students from each class are named Star of the Month for their studies, neatness and behaviour.

Seth Vidyalaya · trips

Out of school

91 students of Classes IV and V visited the Nehru Planetarium and Nehru Museum.

133 of the youngest went to Kalindi Kunj Park, and 106 from Classes II and III visited Waste to Wonder and Indraprastha Park. Volunteers from Deloitte also came 13 times in the year to run activities with the children.

Project · supported by Seth Foundation

Project Savera

300 children, 175 boys and 125 girls, from rag-picker and other poor families of Madanpur Khadar.

A formal school in the afternoons, in Seth Vidyalaya’s building, giving low-cost primary education on the MCD curriculum from Nursery to Class IV. Weaker students get special attention to keep up with their class. All 300 students who sat the annual examination were promoted, and 98 new children joined this year.

Project · for meritorious students

Scholarship Scheme for Meritorious Students

22 students now receive Rs 12,000 a year to carry on studying.

It began in April 2017, when two girls topped their class V. They won places at Lady Irwin School for class VI, but their parents could not pay the transport to such a distant school. So the School Management Committee decided to give the toppers of class V Rs 1,000 a month for a year, towards fees, transport, uniform and books.

Every year since, four new students who come first or second are awarded the scholarship, and it is renewed each year for students who score more than 60% in their final results. Ten individual donors contribute to it every year.

Both schools · parents

Parents and the school

12 parent-teacher meetings at each school, with 70% of parents at Seth Vidyalaya and 74% at Project Savera.

An eighteen-member School Management Committee of parents’ representatives, teachers, trustees and members from educational institutions met three times in the year to review the school’s progress and plan ahead. The Parent Teacher Association met on 11 November 2025 and chose a new parent representative for the committee.
